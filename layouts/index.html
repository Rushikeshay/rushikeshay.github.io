Chicago Tax Increment Financing (TIF) Analysis: How do TIFs impact municipal tax bases?

Analyzes how Chicago's TIF districts affect property values and municipal tax levies, using spatial and longitudinal data to assess a financing tool that redirects billions in public revenue. The project maps district boundaries across the city and examines their relationship with local economic outcomes over time.

Aravalli Range Ruling: Investigative Article

Found that over 96% of the historically understood Aravalli range would not qualify for legal protection under the Supreme Court of India’s November 2025 definition, which introduced new prominence and clustering criteria. Applied these criteria to satellite-derived elevation data using a transparent, replicable methodology that integrates GIS, statistical analysis, and data visualization to translate a technical legal standard into concrete spatial outcomes.

D3.js HTML/CSS Statistical Modeling Geospatial Analysis

Assessing the Environmental and Economic Impacts of Kenya's Feed-in-Tariff Policy

A quasi-experimental evaluation of Kenya's 2008 renewable energy policy using Difference-in-Differences and synthetic control methods. This study quantifies the policy's dual impact on carbon emissions reduction and economic development, comparing Kenya's outcomes against Ghana and 15 other Sub-Saharan African nations to assess whether green energy incentives can deliver both environmental and growth benefits.

Miriam Gold, Seth Binder, Christoph Nolte — Research Assistant: Rushikesh Jadhav

PLOS ONE, September 2023. This study demonstrates an enhanced methodology for estimating fair market values of undeveloped parcels across the United States, incorporating agricultural productivity variables and shifting to USDA farm resource region scales to achieve a ~15% improvement in predictive accuracy and extend coverage to 376 previously unmodeled counties.

About

I am a data analyst and policy researcher with an MPP from the University of Chicago's Harris School of Public Policy. I specialize in turning large-scale administrative data into decisions, with experience working on Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services data, evaluating food security interventions for the World Bank, and modeling rural credit access for farmers in India.

My work spans economic consulting, international development, and public finance, with a focus on causal inference and program evaluation in emerging markets and federal policy contexts.

I work across the full analytical stack — SAS, Stata, R, Python, SQL, and D3.js — building everything from federal deliverables and RCT analyses to interactive data visualizations that make complex findings accessible to policymakers and the public.
