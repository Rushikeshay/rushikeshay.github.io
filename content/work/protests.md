An interactive, web-based tool analyzing worldwide protest events from 2017 to 2025, enabling users to explore protest outcomes, motivating factors, and more. Built with D3.js, the tool transforms a simple dataset from the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace into an interactive resource for policy researchers and analysts tracking global civil society trends.
